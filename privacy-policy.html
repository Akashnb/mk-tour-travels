What we collect

Our website has no sign-up, no contact form and no database. We do not ask you to enter personal details on the site. The only data the site collects automatically is the analytics data described below.

Content from Google

Our photos, rating and reviews come from our Google Business profile. When your browser loads them, Google may receive technical information such as your IP address. We may also keep a temporary copy of this public business information in your browser's local storage so the page loads faster. It contains no personal information about you.

Website analytics

We use Google Analytics (a service of Google LLC) to understand how people use our website, so we can improve it. It records things like the pages you view, which menu items, cars and buttons you tap (for example Call or WhatsApp), how far you scroll, your approximate location (city level), and your device, browser and language. It does not record what you type into WhatsApp, and we do not use it to identify you.

Google Analytics uses cookies for this. To opt out, block cookies in your browser settings or install the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on. Google's own privacy policy explains how it handles this data.

Hosting and logs

Our website host may keep standard server logs, such as IP address and pages requested, for security and to keep the site working.

Cookies

Apart from the Google Analytics cookies described above, we do not set cookies. We do not use advertising or remarketing cookies.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete the details of any past booking enquiry by calling or messaging us. We keep trip details only as long as needed to provide the service and meet legal or accounting needs.
